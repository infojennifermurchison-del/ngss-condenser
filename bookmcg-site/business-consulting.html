Compliance · Technical Assistance

Make sure this isn't your last award.

Winning was step one. We help you implement the award, monitor performance, and measure outcomes — so you stay compliant, deliver what you promised, and position for the next one.

What this door opens

Post-award technical assistance for grantees and contractors. We turn your award conditions and performance measures into systems you can actually run — implementation plans, monitoring routines, and outcome data that holds up to funders and auditors.

Implementation support

Stand up the plan, staffing, and workflows your award requires — from kickoff to steady state.

Monitoring & compliance

Routines and documentation that keep you inside the rules and ready for any review or audit.

Outcome measurement

Define, track, and report the metrics your funder cares about — with data you can defend.

Renewal & re-award readiness

Build the track record and reporting that make renewal and the next award the obvious choice.

Who it's for

  • Grantees and contractors who just won and now have to perform
  • Programs accountable for outcomes, audits, or performance measures
  • Organizations that struggled to report or hit targets last time
  • Leaders who want this award to lead to the next one
