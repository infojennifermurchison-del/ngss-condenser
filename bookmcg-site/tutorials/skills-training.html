Skills Training

Executive Functioning and Skills Training.

Executive functioning tutorials and behavioral health skills training for people diagnosed with ADD and ADHD, and related conditions. Practical skills for everyday life, provided by a licensed professional counselor.

Request a Consultation

Who it's for

Skills training for people diagnosed with ADD or ADHD, and related conditions such as autism spectrum disorder, anxiety, depression, or severe emotional disturbance, who want practical skills they can use in everyday life.

What's included

  • Executive functioning skills for ADD and ADHD
  • Coping skills training
  • Social skills development
  • Emotional regulation skills
  • Daily living skills for behavioral conditions

Often billable to insurance in Texas

Behavioral Health Skills Training (BHST) is typically reimbursed by Texas Medicaid, Medicare, and many commercial insurers when it is provided by a licensed mental health professional.

Coverage and benefits vary by plan. We help you verify your benefits before you begin, and we cannot guarantee reimbursement.

Meet your counselor

Your counselor

Elizabeth W. Sims, MA, LPC

Licensed Professional Counselor.

I bring a unique blend of empathy, evidence based strategies and real world insight into the lives of those with difficulties navigating depression, anxiety, emotional dysregulation and life's transitions, meeting clients where they are and helping them to connect or reconnect with their inner strengths through integration of techniques from cognitive behavioral therapy, mindfulness and trauma informed care, tailoring to individual needs and goals, guiding towards emotional resilience and self awareness, needed to move forward with clarity, confidence and insight which emulates 2020 vision. I offer space for transformation.

Communication is key to establishing trust, promoting respect, safety and acceptance. Validation is important as well to give clients a safe place to share and eventually explore unhealthy and unhelpful patterns in behavior and thought.

As a young child I felt a natural connection to the elderly and small children, the stories, the innocence, the helplessness. As an educator, celebrating milestones in the lives of students and parents was very rewarding. So, I not only bring credentials, but real world empathy through a trauma informed lens, shaped by experience, centered around you.
