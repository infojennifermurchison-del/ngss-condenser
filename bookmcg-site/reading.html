Reading Tutoring

Reading Tutoring That Closes the Gap.

Individual and small group reading tutoring for kindergarten through grade 4, virtual and in person in northwest Houston.

Book a Free Reading Check

Who it's for

Reading tutoring for students in kindergarten through grade 4 who are behind in reading, including students who did not meet grade level on the grade 3 STAAR reading test.

How it works

1

A free reading check to find exact gaps.

2

A plan built for your child.

3

Short sessions with progress checks, and a written update for parents after every session.

What we teach

  • Phonological awareness
  • Phonics and decoding
  • Fluency
  • Vocabulary
  • Comprehension

Taught explicitly and systematically, following the science of teaching reading and aligned to the Texas reading standards.

Schedule and format

When

After school, evenings, and Saturdays.

Where

Virtual statewide, or in person in northwest Houston.

Using PASS funds

Texas families whose child did not meet grade level on the grade 3 reading STAAR may qualify for $400 through the Texas Parent Access to Supplemental Supports (PASS) program to pay for tutoring. Families apply at pass.tea.texas.gov.
