Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Murchison Consulting Group, LLC (“Murchison Consulting Group,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit bookmcg.com (the “Site”), book a call, or engage our services. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us. Your name, email address, phone number, business details, and anything you share when you book a call, complete a form, or communicate with us.
  • Booking and payment information. When you book a paid strategy call, your scheduling and payment details are collected and processed by our third-party booking and payment providers. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers.
  • Automatically collected information. Standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages viewed, which may be gathered through cookies or similar technologies.

2. How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, and deliver the calls and services you request;
  • To process payments and apply your booking fee toward services;
  • To respond to your questions and communicate about your engagement;
  • To improve the Site and our services;
  • To send updates or offers you have asked to receive (you can opt out at any time);
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate — for example, our scheduling calendar, payment processor, email and CRM tools, and analytics providers — and only as needed to perform those functions. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Cookies and analytics

The Site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how the Site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some features.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide services, meet legal and accounting requirements, and resolve disputes. When it is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

6. Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of marketing communications at any time. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond consistent with applicable law.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

The Site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent version, and changes take effect when posted.
