About the firm

Jennifer E. Murchison, M.Ed.

Founder & Principal Consultant of Murchison Consulting Group — a learning, leadership, and execution leader with 23 years translating executive priorities into capability that holds up under pressure.

Murchison Consulting Group builds leader and operator capacity in regulated, outcome-measured environments. We work with the people accountable for performance outcomes — childcare directors, workforce providers, education grantees, small-business owners, and the prime contractors who serve them — and turn compliance baselines and hard-won expertise into results that get measured: star ratings, audit outcomes, funding awarded, contracts retained, and revenue gained.

The experience behind the work

Jennifer brings a rare combination of curriculum architecture, adult learning design, leadership coaching, performance improvement, and data-driven transformation. She has done the work at scale and at the top of the org chart:

  • Chief Academic Officer of Bridgeport Public Schools — Connecticut's largest district, serving roughly 19,000 students — leading district-wide curriculum, assessment, and professional learning.
  • Campus and district leader across Texas, delivering an 86% reduction in dropouts, a failing campus turned to a B rating in eight months, and principal effectiveness gains up to 67%.
  • Title I District Coordinator managing $14M in federal funds at 100% compliance.
  • Founder of Murchison Consulting Group, scaling a multi-service firm across executive coaching, curriculum design, technical assistance, and performance consulting — 250+ leaders served.

How we work

Our methodology is backward-designed from the outcome you are accountable for. We don't perform expertise — we demonstrate it through specificity, using the precise language of the worlds we serve: compliance, designation, performance tier, capacity, and accountability. The result is work that reviewers approve, teams actually use, and owners can build on.

Jennifer designed the RISEhigHER Network — a five-phase business development pathway anchored in adult-learning practice — and builds AI-enabled curriculum and workflow systems that accelerate branded, customized deliverables without losing the human judgment the work requires.
