Run your own numbers first

What is a star level worth to your center?

Before you decide whether this is worth a Saturday, find out what it is worth to your center. The Texas Workforce Commission publishes a Texas Rising Star Revenue Calculator. Enter your board, your ages, your scholarship enrollment, and your current level, and it will tell you what the next star level pays you.

Open the state revenue calculator

This downloads a spreadsheet from the State of Texas.

Example

Say your center serves 45 subsidized children and your 2 star reimbursement runs about $34 a day per child. State law sets a 3 star rate about 2 percent higher for the same care. That is roughly 68 cents more per child each day, about $153 a week, and close to $7,600 a year for moving up one star. Reach 4 star and the gap grows again.

Illustration only. Your real number depends on your board, ages, and enrollment. Run the calculator above for your center.

State law sets the floor. A certified provider's minimum rate must exceed the maximum non certified rate for the same category of care by at least 5 percent at 2 star, 7 percent at 3 star, and 9 percent at 4 star, under Texas Government Code 2308.315.

That number is the reason for this session. Everything below is how you get there.

Why Category 2 and not the others

Look at what it costs to move each category. Category 1 means hiring or credentialing staff. Category 4 means furniture, materials, and facility work. Both require capital, and if you had capital sitting there you would have spent it already.

See the difference

What changes in the classroom

Category 2 is not a form. It is what a teacher does in the small moments with a child. Here is what those moments look like now, and what they look like once you coach them on purpose.

× In classrooms today
  • A meltdown gets "You're okay, go sit down."
  • Cleanup is a rushed "Hurry up, line up."
  • "Did you have fun?" A yes, and the talk stops.
  • Every drawing earns the same "Good job."
  • No one watches the interaction, so it never changes.
✓ After you coach the interactions
  • "You're frustrated. Do you want blocks or books?"
  • Cleanup becomes a counting song the room follows.
  • "What do you think happens next?" opens real talk.
  • "You shared the red cup. Now try offering the blue."
  • Each teacher is coached on one focus and grows.

No false promises

The honest timeline

This is not a rate increase in October. The chain runs in order:

  1. You coach the interactions.
  2. Your Category 2 score moves at your next assessment.
  3. Your star level follows.
  4. Your rate follows that.

Certification work can run up to 24 months from entry level, so where you land depends on where you already sit in your assessment cycle.

Come knowing that. What this session buys you is the ability to start moving the score now instead of after the next assessment tells you what you already suspected.
