Category 2 Teacher Child Interactions carries 40 percent of the Texas Rising Star rating, and it is the category leaders most often manage rather than coach. This full day working session moves directors and mentors from tracking compliance to actively developing the quality of interactions in their classrooms. Participants study real classroom video against the Category 2 rubric, learn to name what they see in specific and coachable terms, and identify the single highest leverage behavior to grow in a room rather than trying to fix everything at once. They then write and rehearse a full coaching conversation using the Six Steps of Effective Feedback, and build a written one quarter coaching cycle with real dates.

Who this is for

Center directors, assistant directors, education coordinators, and Texas Rising Star mentors. This is an intermediate level session for leaders who already carry responsibility for classroom quality and want to coach it, not just monitor it.

What participants will be able to do

  1. Identify the highest leverage Category 2 behavior to coach in a real classroom, using the Texas Rising Star Category 2 rubric.
  2. Run a coaching conversation through the Six Steps of Effective Feedback, ending in a teacher named next step.
  3. Build and run a one quarter coaching cycle that observes every teacher in at least two settings on a single Category 2 focus.

What is included

Every participant receives a working packet, formatted for immediate workplace use:

  • Category 2 rubric reference
  • Name the Interaction card set
  • Coaching Ladder card
  • Category 2 Observation Capture Sheet
  • Watch and Note sheet
  • Six Steps Conversation Plan, with worked example and blank template
  • Murchison Feedback Protocol
  • COACH Cycle planning template
