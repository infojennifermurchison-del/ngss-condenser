Stage 1 · Awareness

Are enough of the right people seeing you?

This is the top of your funnel. If Awareness is broken, nothing else matters — nobody will ever see the rest.

Total views across your primary platform in the last 30 days. Find this in TikTok Studio, Instagram Insights, or your platform's analytics.
Total engagements (likes + comments + shares + saves) divided by views, times 100. Most platforms show this directly in analytics.
Stage 2 · Interest

Is your content making them want more?

People see you — but do they click through, download, or take a next step? This is where most content creators lose 90% of their audience without knowing it.

Of the people who view your content, what percent click through to your link-in-bio or website? Most platforms show this in analytics.
How many people joined your email list in the last 30 days via freebie, newsletter signup, or other opt-in?
Stage 3 · Decision

Are they taking the next step?

People are on your site, product page, or booking page. Are they actually taking action? This is where positioning, pricing, and trust get tested.

How many unique people visit your website, store, or booking page each month?
Of your website visitors, what percent actually book a discovery call? (Booked calls ÷ total visitors × 100)
Of your store visitors, what percent add an item to cart? (Add-to-carts ÷ total visitors × 100) Available in Shopify analytics or Google Analytics.
Stage 4 · Action

Are they buying?

This is where strategy meets revenue. Your sales metrics tell us whether your offer, pricing, and sales process are working.

Your actual revenue from the last 30 days — everything combined (products, services, coaching, etc.).
Of every 10 discovery calls you had in the last 90 days, how many turned into paying clients? (If you had 10 calls and closed 2, that's 20%.)
Of the people who add to cart, what percent actually complete checkout and pay? (Completed orders ÷ add-to-carts × 100) The inverse of cart abandonment — available in Shopify or your payment processor.
The average dollar amount per completed order in the last 30 days. (Total revenue ÷ number of orders)
Stage 5 · Advocacy

Are they coming back and bringing others?

Happy clients fuel predictable growth through repeat purchases, referrals, and reviews. If Stage 5 is empty, you're stuck in a perpetual cold-lead machine.

Of customers who bought in the last 6 months, what percent came back to buy again?
Total written or video reviews.
New clients who came from a referral.
