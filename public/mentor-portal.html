Consent & Authorizations (Forms B–E) — signed here
Form B — Program Consent & Voluntary Participation
📢 Read aloud to the parent/guardian: “This program gives your child mentoring and counseling support to help with school attendance. A mentor or counselor will meet with your child regularly during pull-out sessions, and sometimes at home, with an individual plan and at least two contacts a week. Taking part is completely voluntary — you may decline, skip any activity, or stop at any time, and it will not affect your child's standing at school. What we discuss is kept private, except when the law requires us to report suspected abuse or neglect, when someone is in serious danger, or when a court orders records. Do you understand and agree to your child taking part?”
Form C — FERPA Records Release
📢 Read aloud to the parent/guardian: “To support your child, we're asking your permission for the school to share your child's records with the program — such as attendance, grades, discipline, and behavior or counseling records related to the program. This is the only way the school can legally share those records with us, they are used only to deliver and report on services, and you may cancel this permission in writing at any time. Do you give permission for the school to share these records with our program?”
Form D — Photography / Media (optional)
📢 Read aloud to the parent/guardian: “This part is optional, and your child can fully take part either way. Sometimes we take photos or video during activities, or partner with community groups for events and rewards. You can allow this for public and program use, allow it for our internal records only, or not allow it at all — and you can change your mind in writing at any time. Which would you prefer?”
Form E — Authorization to Release Student from Class
📢 Read aloud to the parent/guardian: “Some sessions happen during the school day. This gives permission for your child to be taken out of class — not out of the building — for a short session, usually about 30 minutes, and then brought right back to class. The assigned mentor is the only person who will pull your child from class, and your child never leaves campus. Once you approve, we will send you a letter you can give the school so the mentor is listed on your child's registration card as allowed to take them out of class but not off campus. Do you give permission for your child to be released from class for these sessions?”
